> **1.3.0** adds a table-of-contents block that shows heading numbers (even in display-only mode), and shows the numbers in the Quiet Outline plugin's panel.
>
> **1.3.0** 新增能显示编号的目录块（仅显示模式也有编号），并让 Quiet Outline 插件的面板也显示编号。

## What's new / 更新内容

**English**

**Table of contents: the `ah-toc` block**

- **Insert table of contents** (command palette and the editor's right-click menu) inserts an `ah-toc` code block. It renders as a table of contents **with the same numbers as the editor** in reading view, Live Preview and exported PDFs. Nothing is written into your note for the contents itself.
- **Level slider.** The inserted block has a slider that fades in when you hover it; drag to choose how deep to expand. When you let go, the choice is saved as a `depth: N` line inside the block (only that line, and only if the block is unchanged since it was drawn).
- Optional `levels: 2-3` line limits which levels are listed. Indentation follows the heading level, one equal step per level, even when a note skips a level.
- **Copy**: select entries and copy to get a numbered, indented outline (same as *Copy numbered outline*); pasting into rich-text apps gives clickable links to the headings. Entries are links in the note, and in exported PDFs.
- Adding or removing headings doesn't shift your view while you type.

**Outline panels**

- Display-only numbers now also show in the **Quiet Outline** plugin's panel (the built-in Outline already did). Nothing to set up.

**中文**

**目录：`ah-toc` 代码块**

- 新增命令 **插入目录**（命令面板和编辑器右键菜单），插入一个 `ah-toc` 代码块。它在阅读视图、实时预览和导出的 PDF 里渲染成目录，**编号与编辑器里一致**；目录本身不会写进你的笔记。
- **层级滑块**：插入的块带滑块，鼠标移上去才淡入；拖动选展开到第几级，松手后把选择存成块里的一行 `depth: N`（只改这一行，而且只在块还和画出来时一致时才写）。
- 可选 `levels: 2-3` 一行限定列出的层级。缩进只看标题级别，每一级一样宽，文章里跳级也一样。
- **复制**：选中几项复制，得到带编号的缩进大纲（与「复制编号大纲」同格式）；粘进富文本应用是指向对应标题的超链接。目录项在笔记里和导出的 PDF 里都是链接。
- 打字时新增 / 删除标题，不会把视图挤动。

**大纲面板**

- 仅显示模式的编号现在也出现在 **Quiet Outline** 插件的面板里（内置大纲早就有），无需设置。
